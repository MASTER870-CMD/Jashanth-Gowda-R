Projects built and tested in the workshop

Three projects covering tool design, machining experiments and mechatronics. Each one has a full specification you can open.

Tool engineering

Design and fabrication of a precision drilling jig

An ergonomic drilling jig for batch production, built around rapid clamping, the 3-2-1 locating principle and hardened drill bushes that stop tool deflection.

Problem
Manual layout marking caused hole concentricity errors and inconsistent cycle times.
Solution
Modeled in SolidWorks, then made with precision milling and heat-treated drill guides.
Outcome
Cut setup time by 40%. Now used as a teaching tool for juniors.
  • SolidWorks
  • GD&T
  • Fitting
CNC and CAM

CNC machining and tool wear optimization

An experimental study of how cutting speed (Vc), feed per tooth (fz) and depth of cut affect end mill flank wear and surface roughness (Ra).

Problem
Rapid carbide cutter wear and unpredictable surface finish in high-volume runs.
Solution
Simulated toolpaths in Mastercam, programmed the G-code and recorded roughness data.
Outcome
35% longer tool life with surface finish held below Ra 1.2 μm.
  • Mastercam
  • CNC milling
  • Metrology
Mechatronics

Autonomous ultrasonic sensor robot car

An autonomous mobile platform with a custom chassis, dual DC motors, ultrasonic distance sensing and collision-avoidance logic.

Problem
Needed a compact, autonomous guidance testbed for moving small parts on the shop floor.
Solution
Combined an HC-SR04 sonar sensor, servo scanning head and differential motor drive.
Outcome
Navigated on its own with a 200 ms obstacle-avoidance response.
  • Arduino
  • Sensors
  • Automation
